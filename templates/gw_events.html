ℹ️ About Gravitational Wave Events

Gravitational wave events occur when two black holes merge, creating ripples in spacetime detected by facilities like LIGO and Virgo. The energy released during these mergers is calculated from the mass difference (mass_1 + mass_2 - final_mass).
